Add Designation

Define a role, assign it to a jurisdiction level, and set reporting

Tip: A designation is a role or post within this department. Assign it to the jurisdiction level where this role exists, and set who this role reports to — either a specific officer designation or an entire office.
infoe.g. District, Taluk
infoThis officer reports to the officer holding this designation
infoThis officer reports directly to the office (Not a specific person)
info Columns required: Level, Designation Title, Short Code, Reports To Type, Reports To Name, Description. Level must match one of this department's real levels. Reports To Type is none, officer, or office — leave Reports To Name blank for "none", otherwise it must match an existing designation (for officer) or office (for office) name.
